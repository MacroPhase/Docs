# Developer & Diagnostic Settings

MacroPhase provides an advanced diagnostic and developer suite for power users, testers, and developers.

## Accessing Developer Settings

You can find developer options in **Settings → Developer Settings**:

- **Targeted Data Wipes**:
  - Wipe Shopping Lists & Pantry Items
  - Wipe AI Chat Conversation History
  - Wipe AI Long-Term Memories
  - Wipe Scanned Product & Search Cache
  - Reset Custom Servings and Category Overrides to factory defaults
- **Simulation & Testing**:
  - Test simulated weight loss/gain phases to preview expenditure calculations.
  - Generate mock food logging history across custom date ranges.
- **Algorithm State Inspector**:
  - Inspect current EWMA and 3-State velocity/acceleration state vectors.
  - View raw EWMA trend weights and expenditure engine confidence values.
