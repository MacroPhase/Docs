# AI Use Cases

The AI coach isn't just a chatbot. It's connected to your data, your food database, and your camera. Here are the most useful things you can do with it.

## Snap a Photo of Food

Point your camera at a plate and the AI identifies what's on it, estimates portions, and logs it to your diary. Works with mixed meals — rice, chicken, vegetables, all in one shot.

1. Tap the camera icon in the Food Log tab
2. Take a photo
3. Review what the AI detected
4. Confirm or adjust portions
5. Log

The scanner uses your configured image model. Gemini's free tier handles this well for most meals.

> 📊 **In MacroPhase**
> You can configure a different model for scanning vs coaching. Use Gemini's free tier for photos (20 requests/day) and a paid model for deeper conversations.

## Create Custom Foods from Photos

Here's a workflow that saves time: snap a photo of a packaged food, let the AI extract the nutrition data, then save it as a custom food with a barcode. Next time you scan that barcode, it pulls up your custom entry instantly.

1. Scan or photograph the nutrition label
2. Ask the coach: "Create a custom food from this label"
3. The AI parses calories, protein, fat, carbs, and serving size
4. Add a barcode to the custom food entry
5. Future scans reference your custom food directly

This is especially useful for local brands or specialty products that aren't in the Open Food Facts database.

## Inline Food Suggestions

The AI can suggest foods based on what you've already eaten today and what you have left. Ask something like:

- "What should I eat to hit my protein target?"
- "I have 400 calories left, suggest something quick"
- "What can I eat that's high in iron?"

The coach checks your remaining macros, your food history, and your preferences before suggesting. It won't recommend foods you've marked as disliked.

## Recipe Generation

Describe a meal and the AI builds a recipe with full nutrition data:

> "Create a recipe: salmon fillet with 200g sweet potato and 150g broccoli"

The coach searches each ingredient, calculates total macros, and saves it to your library. You can then log it like any other food.

## Body Metrics Logging

Ask the coach to log your measurements:

- "Log my body fat as 18%"
- "Record my waist as 82 cm"
- "My chest is 104 cm today"

The coach updates your body metrics for the selected date.

## Weight Trend Analysis

Ask the coach about your weight:

- "How's my weight trend this month?"
- "Am I losing weight consistently?"
- "Why did my weight jump last week?"

The coach queries your weight data, applies trend analysis, and gives you an honest assessment.

## Nutrition History

Ask for summaries over any time period:

- "What's my average protein this week?"
- "How many calories did I eat yesterday?"
- "Show me my micronutrient intake for the past 7 days"

The coach pulls the data and presents it in a digestible format.

## Smart Food Search

Instead of scrolling through search results, ask the coach to find specific foods:

- "Find a protein bar under 200 calories"
- "What Greek yogurt brands do I usually eat?"
- "Search for low-carb bread options"

The coach searches the database with context — it knows what you've eaten before and what fits your current macros.

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> The AI is most useful when you combine it with your data. "Suggest a snack" is generic. "I have 300 calories and 25g of protein remaining, suggest something with chicken" is powerful. Give it context and it delivers.
