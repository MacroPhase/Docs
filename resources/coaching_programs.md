# Coaching Programs

MacroPhase offers three coaching programs that control how your targets are set and adjusted. The program you choose determines how much control you have versus how much the app handles automatically.

## Coached Mode

The app makes all the decisions. You log food and weight, complete your weekly check-in, and the app adjusts your targets based on your progress.

- **Calorie targets** — Set by the app based on your TDEE and goal
- **Macro targets** — Calculated from your protein tier and diet style
- **Adjustments** — Automatic weekly (capped at 200 kcal)
- **Check-ins** — Required weekly, the app recommends changes

**Best for:** People who want a hands-off experience. You focus on logging, the app handles the math.

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Coached mode uses your actual data — not just your initial profile. As you log more, the recommendations get more accurate. The app learns your real expenditure and adjusts accordingly.

## Collaborative Mode

You set the targets, but the app gives you recommendations. A middle ground between full automation and full control.

- **Calorie targets** — You set them, but the app suggests changes during check-ins
- **Macro targets** — You configure them, with guidance from the app
- **Adjustments** — You approve or override the app's suggestions
- **Check-ins** — The app analyzes your data and recommends adjustments

**Best for:** People who want to understand the process and make their own decisions, but appreciate having a second opinion.

## Manual Mode

You set everything. The app tracks your data and shows you the metrics, but doesn't make any recommendations.

- **Calorie targets** — You set them manually
- **Macro targets** — You configure the split
- **Adjustments** — None, you handle it yourself
- **Check-ins** — Optional, the app still calculates your TDEE

**Best for:** Experienced users who already know their numbers and just want accurate tracking without the coaching layer.

## How to Switch

1. Go to **More** tab
2. Tap **Program Style** under Feature Settings
3. Select your mode
4. Confirm the change

Switching programs doesn't affect your logged data. Your food logs, weight entries, and history stay intact.

> ⚠️ **Common Myth**
> "Manual mode is for advanced users." Not necessarily. If you already know your calorie target from another app or coach, Manual mode lets you plug it in without the app overriding it. It's about preference, not expertise.

## What Stays the Same

Regardless of your program:

- Your TDEE is always calculated from your data
- Your food log and weight history are always tracked
- The AI coach is always available
- Your memory system continues learning
- Check-ins are always optional

The program only affects **how targets are set and adjusted** — not the underlying data or features.

## Program Styles and Macros

When you choose a program, you also configure your macro split:

- **Balanced** — Equal-ish distribution across protein, fat, carbs
- **High Carb / Low Fat** — More carbs, less fat
- **Low Carb / High Fat** — More fat, less carbs
- **Keto** — Very low carb, high fat
- **Custom** — Set your own percentages

These styles determine the minimum floors for each macro. You can always override them manually.
