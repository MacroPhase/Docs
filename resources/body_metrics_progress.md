# Body Metrics & Progress Photos

The scale measures one thing: your total body weight. It doesn't tell you how much of that weight is muscle, fat, water, or a combination. Body metrics and progress photos fill that gap.

## What You Can Track

### Body Measurements

- **Body fat %** — The most direct indicator of fat loss vs muscle loss
- **Waist** — Strong correlate of abdominal fat
- **Chest** — Tracks upper body changes
- **Hips** — Tracks lower body changes
- **Arms** — Tracks muscle gain
- **Thighs** — Tracks leg development
- **Neck, shoulders, forearms, calves** — For the detail-oriented

All optional. Track what matters to you.

### Progress Photos

Three angles: front, side, back.

> **Tips for consistent photos:**
> - Same lighting each time
> - Same time of day (morning, before eating)
> - Same pose and distance from camera
> - Similar clothing (or none) for easy comparison
> - Don't flex — you want an honest baseline

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Photos reveal changes that the scale and tape measure miss. A 2-week period where the scale barely moved might show visible visual progress in photos. The scale can't differentiate between fat loss and muscle gain — your eyes can.

## Where to Find It

- **Dashboard** — Body Metrics cards show latest weight and body fat %. Tap to open.
- **More → Body Metrics** — Full measurement and photo screen.

## How Body Fat % Affects Calculations

If you enter a body fat percentage, MacroPhase uses the Katch-McArdle formula for BMR estimation, which is more accurate than Mifflin-St Jeor for people who know their composition.

> **Where to get a reading:**
> - DEXA scan (most accurate, ~$50-100)
> - Hydrostatic weighing
> - Skinfold calipers (practice required)
> - Bioelectrical impedance (less accurate but consistent under same conditions)

## How Often?

- **Body fat %:** Every 2-4 weeks
- **Circumference measurements:** Every 2-4 weeks
- **Progress photos:** Every 4 weeks

Consistency matters more than frequency. Same day, same conditions, each cycle.

## Storage

Photos are stored locally on your device in the app's private directory. They're never uploaded or shared. You can delete individual photos or all photos from the Body Metrics screen.
