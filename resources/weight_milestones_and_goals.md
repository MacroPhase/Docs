# Weight Milestones & Goal Projections

Reaching a major fitness or weight goal is a marathon. MacroPhase breaks your long-term journey into actionable, short-term milestones to keep you motivated and accurate.

## Dynamic Weight Milestones

When you set a target weight in your strategy, MacroPhase divides the gap between your starting weight and your final target into incremental milestone checkpoints (e.g., every 2.5 kg or 5 lbs):

- **Progressive Checkpoints**: Highlights your next immediate milestone instead of only focusing on a distant end goal.
- **Celebration Badges**: When your trend weight crosses a milestone threshold, MacroPhase celebrates your achievement with milestone badges.
- **Rate-Adjusted Target Dates**: Your projected completion dates update dynamically based on your actual 3-State velocity (rate of change in kg/week), giving you realistic expectations.

---

## Goal Pacing & Expenditure Adaptation

Unlike static diet apps that calculate a single calorie deficit and stick to it forever:

- If your expenditure naturally slows down during a fat loss phase (adaptive thermogenesis), your weekly check-in will suggest small, sustainable adjustments.
- The V3 expenditure algorithm uses velocity and acceleration from your 3-State weight trend to ignore temporary scale spikes and prevent sudden, aggressive calorie cuts.
