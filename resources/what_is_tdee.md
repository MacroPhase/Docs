# What Is TDEE?

If you eat 2,500 calories per day and your weight stays exactly the same for months, your TDEE is probably close to 2,500 calories.

That's it. No metabolism magic. No secret fat-burning trick. Just energy in versus energy out.

**TDEE — Total Daily Energy Expenditure** — is the total number of calories your body burns in a single day. It's the most important number in nutrition, because it tells you how much energy you actually need.

## Where Your Calories Go

Your body burns calories through four channels:

### Keeping You Alive (BMR) — 60-70%

Your brain, heart, lungs, and cells burn calories just existing. Breathing, circulating blood, maintaining body temperature — all of it costs energy. This is your **Basal Metabolic Rate**, and it's by far the biggest piece of the puzzle.

> 📊 **In MacroPhase**
> MacroPhase estimates your BMR using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (weight, height, age, sex). If you've entered a body fat percentage, it uses the Katch-McArdle formula instead, which is more accurate because it accounts for lean body mass.

### Digesting Food (TEF) — ~10%

Your body burns calories processing what you eat. Protein has the highest thermic effect — 20-30% of protein calories are burned during digestion. Fat and carbs are processed more efficiently.

This is one reason high-protein diets help with fat loss: you literally absorb fewer net calories from protein than from the same number of calories from carbs or fat.

### Moving Without Trying (NEAT) — 15-20%

Fidgeting, walking to your car, typing, standing, taking the stairs. All the movement that isn't deliberate exercise. NEAT varies dramatically between people and is one of the biggest reasons two people of the same size can have different TDEEs.

### Exercise (EAT) — Variable

The calories burned during intentional workouts. Ranges from zero on rest days to thousands for endurance athletes.

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> NEAT — the non-exercise movement — is often the hidden variable in weight loss. Two people with identical BMRs can have TDEEs that differ by 300+ calories based purely on daily movement habits.

## Why Static Calculators Go Stale

Online TDEE calculators give you a number based on population averages. They're a starting point, but they can't account for your individual metabolism, NEAT, or how your body responds to dieting.

After 12 weeks of dieting, a calculator estimate might be off by 200-400 calories. That's the difference between losing weight and wondering why nothing's happening.

This is why MacroPhase calculates TDEE from your actual data. Your food logs and weight trends tell the real story.

## How TDEE Changes Over Time

Your TDEE isn't fixed. It shifts with:

- **Body weight** — Heavier bodies burn more calories
- **Body composition** — Muscle burns more calories than fat at rest
- **Activity level** — More movement = higher TDEE
- **Dieting history** — Prolonged deficits can reduce expenditure
- **Sleep and stress** — Both affect hormonal regulation of energy burn

> ⚠️ **Common Myth**
> "My metabolism is broken." It almost certainly isn't. Metabolic adaptation during dieting is real but modest — typically 5-15% below expected. The more common explanation for stalls is underreporting food intake or overestimating exercise calories.
