# Check-Ins Explained

You've been logging food and weight for a week. Now it's time for your check-in — the moment MacroPhase looks at your data and asks: "Is the plan still working?"

## When Check-Ins Happen

Weekly, on a day you choose (configurable in Notifications). You'll get a reminder.

## What Actually Happens

### 1. The App Reviews Your Data

MacroPhase looks at:

- **Weight change** — Did you move toward your goal?
- **Average intake** — Were you hitting your targets?
- **TDEE confidence** — How much data fed the estimate?
- **Logging consistency** — How complete was your food diary?

### 2. You Get a Recommendation

- **No change** — You're on track. Keep going.
- **Increase calories** — You're losing faster than planned, or your expenditure went up.
- **Decrease calories** — Progress slowed more than expected.
- **Low data warning** — Not enough information to make a confident recommendation.

### 3. You Decide

You can accept, override, or skip. If you skip, your current targets stay unchanged.

## The 200 kcal Rule

Adjustments are capped at 200 kcal per week. This prevents whiplash.

If the math says "reduce by 500 kcal," the app recommends 200 kcal. Over 2-3 weeks, the target converges to the right level without jarring jumps.

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Small, frequent adjustments beat large, infrequent ones. A 200 kcal tweak every week for three weeks gets you to the right place more sustainably than a 600 kcal swing all at once.

## Calorie Floors

Even if the math calls for very low calories, the floor is enforced:

- 90% of your BMR (minimum 1,000 kcal)
- 800 kcal absolute minimum

> ⚠️ **Common Myth**
> "Just cut more calories." There's a floor for a reason. Very low calorie diets cause muscle loss, metabolic suppression, nutrient deficiencies, and hormonal disruption. MacroPhase keeps you above the danger zone.

## What If I Skip?

Your targets stay where they were. The TDEE continues updating in the background, but your calorie and macro numbers don't change until you complete a check-in or manually adjust them.

## Confidence and Data Quality

The confidence indicator reflects four factors:

- **Coverage** — % of days with food logs in the past 14 days
- **Freshness** — How recent your last log was
- **Stability** — Consistency of your patterns
- **Depth** — How much total history exists

> 📊 **In MacroPhase**
> Low confidence doesn't mean your plan is wrong. It means the app is less certain about your TDEE. Log more consistently for a week and confidence improves. The system is designed to get better with more data.

## What If I'm Not Losing?

If the scale and trend aren't moving after 2+ weeks:

1. **Check adherence** — Are you actually hitting your targets?
2. **Review logging accuracy** — Are you measuring portions or guessing?
3. **Look at the trend, not the scale** — Is the scale noisy but the trend stable?
4. **Wait for the next check-in** — If progress truly stalled, it will catch it

> **Real talk:** Studies show people underestimate calories by 20-50% on average. A food scale is the single most impactful tool for accuracy. If you're guessing portions, you're probably eating more than you think.
