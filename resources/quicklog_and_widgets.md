# QuickLog AI & Home Screen Widgets

MacroPhase lets you track food and interact with the AI Coach in seconds without navigating through multiple menus.

## QuickLog AI: Natural Language Logging

The QuickLog bar in your Food Log uses intelligent natural language processing to turn everyday meal descriptions into precise logged foods:

- **Type Naturally**: Enter statements like:
  - *"2 large eggs, 2 slices of whole wheat toast, and 1 tbsp butter"*
  - *"300g greek yogurt with 50g blueberries and 15g honey for breakfast"*
  - *"Double scoop whey protein shake with 300ml whole milk"*
- **Automatic Breakdown**: QuickLog splits multi-item sentences into individual foods, searches the offline database for exact macro values, and calculates the exact grams and calories.
- **Instant Undo**: Whenever you log with QuickLog, a system notification with an **Undo** action appears, allowing you to revert accidental logs in one tap.

## Home Screen Glance Widgets

Keep track of your day directly from your Android home screen:

### 1. Quick Ask Coach Widget
- Tapping this widget launches the AI Coach with immediate keyboard focus or voice input so you can ask nutrition questions, check remaining targets, or log on the fly.

### 2. Macro & Calorie Ring Widget
- Shows your remaining calories and macro progress (Protein, Fat, Carbs) with live visual rings.
- Updates automatically throughout the day as you log meals.

### 3. Weight Progress Widget
- Displays your current trend weight, current phase goal, and progress toward your target weight at a glance.

> 📱 **How to Add Widgets**
> Long-press on any empty area of your Android home screen → tap **Widgets** → scroll to **MacroPhase** → drag your chosen widget onto your home screen.
