# Understanding Your Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen — a snapshot of where you stand today and how the week is going. Here's what everything means.

## Top Section: The Pager

Three swipable views at the top:

### Weekly View
A 7-day grid showing each day's calorie and macro progress. Each column is one day with colored bars for protein, fat, and carbs. The right panel shows your targets versus what you've consumed.

### Daily Nutrition View
A ring or tank showing today's calorie progress — how much eaten versus remaining.

### Energy Balance View
The relationship between intake and expenditure — whether you're in surplus or deficit relative to your TDEE.

Swipe between these for different perspectives on the same data.

## Insights Section

- **Expenditure card** — Your current TDEE estimate with a sparkline
- **Weight Trend card** — Your smoothed trend weight with a sparkline

These update as you log more data. The confidence indicator shows reliability.

## AI Insights

If enabled, the AI generates periodic insight cards highlighting patterns — unusual expenditure changes, weight trend shifts, or adherence patterns.

## Habits Section

- **Weigh-In** — 30-day grid of weight logging consistency
- **Food Logging** — 30-day grid of food logging consistency

Green dots mean you logged that day. Visual motivation to stay consistent.

## Body Metrics

- **Scale Weight** — Latest entry with 30-day sparkline
- **Body Fat %** — Latest reading if recorded

Tap either for the full Body Metrics screen.

## Nutrition Section

Detailed nutrient cards showing daily defaults: calories, protein, fat, carbs. Tap **See All** for up to 24 nutrients including fiber, sodium, vitamins, and minerals.

## Customizing

Tap **Customize Dashboard** or long-press sections to rearrange, show, or hide widgets. Drag to reorder. Choose between ring and tank visualizations.

Layout is saved per-device.
