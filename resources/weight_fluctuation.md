# Why Weight Fluctuates Daily

Imagine you weigh 80 kg on Friday.

Saturday: pizza with friends.

Sunday: 81.7 kg.

Did you gain 1.7 kg of fat overnight?

No. That would require roughly 13,000 excess calories — about 20 slices of pizza above your maintenance. What actually happened is far more boring and far less scary.

## What's Really Going On

Your body is mostly water. And water weight is wildly volatile.

### Water Retention

- **Sodium:** A single salty restaurant meal can add 1-3 lbs of water weight. Your body holds onto water to dilute the excess sodium. It resolves in 1-2 days.
- **Carbohydrates:** Each gram of glycogen (stored carbs) binds ~3 grams of water. A high-carb meal fills glycogen stores — and brings water with it.
- **Hormones:** Cortisol, estrogen, and progesterone all affect water balance. Women often see 2-5 lb fluctuations around menstrual cycles.
- **Stress:** Elevated cortisol increases water retention.

### Food Volume

A large meal doesn't vanish instantly. It sits in your stomach and intestines for hours. A restaurant dinner can weigh 1-3 lbs until fully digested. Eating late means undigested food is still present at your morning weigh-in.

### Exercise

Intense exercise causes temporary muscle inflammation, which increases water retention. Post-workout carb consumption refills glycogen (and its associated water). Regular exercise also increases blood volume over time.

### Bowel Contents

The simple presence or absence of waste in your digestive tract accounts for 0.5-2 lbs.

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Daily weight can fluctuate 2-5 lbs (0.9-2.3 kg) in healthy adults. This is completely normal and almost never reflects actual fat gain or loss.

> ⚠️ **Common Myth**
> "I gained weight after eating pizza." You gained water, food volume, and glycogen. Fat gain requires a sustained calorie surplus over days and weeks, not one meal. Give your body 2-3 days to process the fluctuation before drawing conclusions.

## Tips for Better Weigh-Ins

1. **Same time, every time** — Morning, after the bathroom, before eating
2. **Trust the trend** — MacroPhase handles this for you
3. **Expect swings** — A 2 lb daily change is noise
4. **Weekly averages tell the story** — If the 7-day average is trending right, you're on track
5. **Don't panic after salty meals** — The water weight passes in 1-2 days

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Your body doesn't know it's Tuesday. It only responds to long-term energy balance. One day's number means almost nothing. Two weeks of data means everything.
