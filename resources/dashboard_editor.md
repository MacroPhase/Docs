# Customizing Your Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen, and you can rearrange it to show the information that matters most to you.

## How to Customize

1. Go to the **Dashboard**
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Tap **Customize Dashboard**
4. Drag sections to reorder
5. Toggle sections on or off
6. Choose between visualization styles (ring vs tank for daily nutrition)

Changes save automatically.

## Available Sections

- **Primary Focus** — The top pager with weekly, daily, and energy balance views
- **Insights** — Expenditure and weight trend sparklines
- **AI Insights** — Periodic analysis from the AI
- **AI Coach** — Coach card with focus mode and memory count
- **Habits** — 30-day logging consistency grids
- **Body Metrics** — Scale weight and body fat % cards
- **Nutrition** — Detailed daily nutrient cards (expandable)
- **More** — Links to dashboard customization and body metrics

## Visualization Options

For the Daily Nutrition view in the top pager:

- **Ring** — Circular progress showing calories consumed vs target
- **Tank** — Bar visualization showing macro fill levels

## Layout Tips

- **Most important stuff at the top** — Drag the sections you check most frequently to the top
- **Hide what you don't use** — If you don't track body metrics, hide that section
- **Keep it clean** — Too many sections makes the Dashboard feel cluttered. Start with the defaults and add only what you need

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Your Dashboard layout is personal. A power user who tracks everything will have a different layout than someone who just logs food and checks weight. Both are valid. Customize for your workflow.

> 📊 **In MacroPhase**
> The Dashboard layout is serialized to JSON and stored per-device. If you use multiple devices, you'll need to customize each one separately. Your data (food logs, weight, etc.) syncs across devices, but the UI layout doesn't.
