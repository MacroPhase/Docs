# Creating Foods & Recipes with AI

MacroPhase includes a multi-step creation wizard that uses AI vision and language models to make creating custom foods and recipes effortless.

## Creating Custom Foods with AI

When logging an unlisted packaged item or restaurant meal:

1. Go to **More → Custom Foods → + Create with AI**.
2. **Front & Back Label Snap**:
   - **Front Photo**: Captures the brand name, product name, and flavor.
   - **Back Photo (Nutrition Panel)**: Captures serving size in grams, calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, and micronutrients.
3. **AI Extraction**: The vision model analyzes both images simultaneously, cross-verifies macro math, and populates the form.
4. **Tagging & Emoji**: Assign custom tags (e.g. *High Protein*, *Snacks*, *Zero Calorie*) and pick a custom emoji.

---

## Creating Recipes with AI

Turn home-cooked meals, online recipe blogs, or photos of food into precise, reusable recipes:

1. Go to **More → Recipes → + Create with AI**.
2. **Photo or Text Description**:
   - Take a picture of your cooked meal or ingredients on the cutting board.
   - Or paste the ingredient text / recipe notes (e.g., *"450g 93/7 ground beef, 1 cup cooked jasmine rice, 15ml olive oil, 1 diced bell pepper"*).
3. **Ingredient Breakdown**: AI parses ingredients, assigns them to verified database items, and sums total prepared weight and macronutrients.
4. **Serving Splits**: Divide by total servings (e.g., 4 meal prep containers) or log by custom gram portions.
