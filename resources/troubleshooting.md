# Troubleshooting

Common issues and fixes.

## AI Features

**"API key invalid"**
- Check you copied the full key (no spaces, no missing chars)
- Verify the key is active on your provider's dashboard
- Some providers need credits first
- Try generating a new key

**Coach not responding**
- Check internet (required except for Local Gemma)
- Try a different model in AI Settings
- Check if your provider is having outages
- Restart the app

**Slow responses**
- Free tiers have rate limits — wait and retry
- Try a faster model (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite` or `deepseek-v4-flash`)
- Start a new conversation if history is long

**Scanner not recognizing foods**
- Photo from directly above
- Good lighting, no shadows
- Separate items on the plate
- Try "Precision" mode in AI Settings

## Food Logging

**Can't find a food**
- Try different terms ("chicken breast" or just "chicken")
- Check offline database packs (**More → Food Databases**)
- Use barcode scanner for packaged foods
- Create a custom food from the label
- Ask the AI coach to search

**Nutrition values wrong**
- Check if it's per-100g or per-serving
- Verify serving size matches what you ate
- Barcode scanner is more accurate for packaged foods

**Can't log to previous day**
- Tap the date at the top of the Food Log tab
- Navigate to the past day
- Tap + to add food

## Weight Tracking

**Scale fluctuating a lot**
- Normal — see "Why Weight Fluctuates Daily"
- Focus on trend weight, not daily swings
- Weigh same time each day
- Look at weekly averages

**Trend not updating**
- Needs regular weigh-ins to converge
- Give it 5-7 days if you haven't weighed recently

## Check-Ins

**Check-in not appearing**
- Check your check-in day in Notifications Settings
- Need 5+ days of data in past 14 days
- Verify notifications are enabled

**Target too low or high**
- Check confidence indicator
- Review logging accuracy
- Next check-in will adjust (capped at 200 kcal)
- Manually adjust in Strategy screen anytime

## Data & Backup

**Can't import backup**
- Ensure it's a MacroPhase export (not MacroFactor — use separate import)
- Try re-exporting from old device
- Try "Merge" mode instead of "Replace"

**Photos not saving**
- Check camera/storage permissions
- Android 13+: check photo/media permissions
- Verify storage space

## General

**App slow or crashing**
- Clear cache (Android Settings → Apps → MacroPhase → Storage → Clear Cache)
- Update to latest version
- Check storage space
- Restart device

**Can't find a feature**
- Search bar at bottom of More tab
- Check Resources section
- Ask the AI coach — it knows MacroPhase features
