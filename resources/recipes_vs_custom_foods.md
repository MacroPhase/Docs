# Recipes vs Custom Foods

MacroPhase has two types of saved foods: **Custom Foods** (single items you've created) and **Recipes** (multi-ingredient meals). They serve different purposes and are stored locally in your database.

## Custom Foods

A custom food is a single item with its own nutrition data. Use it for:

- Local brands not in the offline database
- Packaged snacks or restaurant items
- Single-ingredient staples with verified nutrition

### How to Create

1. Go to **More → Custom Foods → + Create**
2. Choose your creation method:
   - **Create with AI (Fastest)**: Snap the front photo and back nutrition label; AI populates all nutrients automatically.
   - **Quick Manual Entry**: Enter calories, protein, carbs, fat, brand name, and custom tags.
3. Pick a custom emoji and assign tags (e.g., *High Protein*, *Zero Calorie*).
4. Save.

---

## Recipes

A recipe is a collection of ingredients with individual nutrition data that calculates total dish weight and per-serving macros. Use it for:

- Multi-component meals (stir-fry, pasta dishes, smoothie bowls)
- Meal prep batches divided into portions or weighed servings
- Home cooking where you want to tweak individual ingredients later

### How to Create

1. Go to **More → Recipes → + Create Recipe**
2. Choose your creation method:
   - **Create with AI**: Snap a picture of your dish or paste ingredient notes. AI will extract and match ingredients to database items.
   - **Manual Recipe Builder**: Search and add ingredients, specify quantities in grams, and set serving sizes.
3. Choose how to divide the recipe:
   - **By Servings** (e.g. 4 meal prep containers)
   - **By Total Prepared Weight** (e.g. log exact grams from the bowl)
4. Save.

---

## Key Differences

| Feature | Custom Foods | Recipes |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | Single item, nutrition per 100g / serving | Multi-ingredient collection with total weight |
| **Logging** | Logs as 1 entry on your timeline | Logs as individual ingredients or a single merged recipe |
| **Creation** | Front/back label photo AI or manual | Meal photo / description AI or ingredient search |
| **Tagging & Emojis** | Custom tags and custom emoji | Custom tags, emojis, and recurring meal schedules |
| **Barcodes** | Can store custom barcodes for instant scan | Multi-ingredient (no single barcode) |

## AI Coach Integration

You can ask the AI Coach to create either type directly from chat:

- *"Create a custom food: Oikos Pro Strawberry Greek Yogurt, 140 cal, 20g protein, 3g fat, 8g carbs per cup"*
- *"Save a recipe for my post-workout shake: 40g whey protein, 300ml milk, 1 banana, 30g peanut butter"*
