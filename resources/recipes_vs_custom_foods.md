# Recipes vs Custom Foods

MacroPhase has two types of saved foods: **Custom Foods** (single items you've created) and **Recipes** (multi-ingredient meals). They serve different purposes and are stored separately.

## Custom Foods

A custom food is a single item with its own nutrition data. Use it for:

- Local brands not in the database
- Homemade items with specific nutrition (your protein shake recipe, your overnight oats)
- Restaurant meals you eat regularly
- Anything you want to save with custom macros

### How to Create

1. Go to **Library** tab
2. Tap **+ Create Custom Food**
3. Enter name, brand (optional), and nutrition per 100g
4. Optionally add a barcode for future scans
5. Save

### When to Use

- You eat a specific product regularly and want to log it quickly
- The database entry for a food has wrong nutrition data
- You want to save a food with a barcode for scanning

## Recipes

A recipe is a collection of ingredients with individual nutrition data. Use it for:

- Multi-component meals (stir-fry, pasta dishes, smoothie bowls)
- Meal prep batches
- Anything you'd describe as "a recipe" rather than "a food"

### How to Create

1. Go to **Library** tab
2. Tap **+ Create Recipe**
3. Add ingredients by searching the food database
4. Set serving sizes for each ingredient
5. Save

### When to Use

- The meal has 2+ distinct ingredients
- You want each ingredient logged separately on your timeline
- You want to adjust individual ingredient portions when logging

## Key Differences

- **Custom Foods** — Single item, single entry in your library. Nutrition per 100g.
- **Recipes** — Multiple ingredients, each with its own nutrition. When logged, each ingredient becomes a separate food log entry.

### Logging Behavior

- **Custom Food** — Logs as one entry. You set the serving size.
- **Recipe** — Logs as multiple entries (one per ingredient). Each shows on your timeline separately. You can edit individual ingredients after logging.

### Barcode Support

- **Custom Foods** — Can store a barcode for scanning
- **Recipes** — No barcode (recipes are multi-ingredient)

> 📊 **In MacroPhase**
> Both appear in the Library tab. Custom Foods show with a generic emoji. Recipes show with their recipe emoji. Both open the PremiumAddFoodSheet when tapped, letting you log them like any other food.

## The AI Can Help

Ask the coach to create either type:

- "Create a custom food: My Protein Shake, 250 cal, 30g protein, 8g fat, 12g carbs per serving"
- "Create a recipe: Chicken Bowl with 200g chicken breast, 150g rice, 100g black beans"

The AI searches the database for ingredients and builds the entry for you.

## Editing and Deleting

Both custom foods and recipes can be edited or deleted from the Library tab. Changes to a recipe **do not** affect previously logged entries — past logs keep the nutrition values they had when logged.
