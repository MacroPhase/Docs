# Why Targets Change Over Time

If you've been using MacroPhase for a few weeks, you might notice your calorie targets shifting slightly after check-ins. This is one of the app's most important features.

## The Problem

Most nutrition apps give you a calorie target from a calculator and never update it. But your body changes:

- As you lose weight, your TDEE drops (smaller body = fewer calories burned)
- As you build muscle, TDEE may rise
- Dieting itself can reduce expenditure beyond what weight loss alone predicts
- Stress, sleep, and activity all shift the numbers

A static target becomes progressively less accurate. After 12 weeks of dieting, it might be off by 200-400 kcal.

## How MacroPhase Adapts

### Continuous TDEE Tracking

Every time you log food and weight, MacroPhase recalculates your TDEE using a rolling 14-day window. Your expenditure estimate always reflects your current state, not your state from two months ago.

### Weekly Check-Ins

Each check-in reviews your actual weight change, average intake, and TDEE confidence. If progress deviates from the plan, the check-in recommends an adjustment.

### Guardrails

- **Max change:** 200 kcal per week
- **Min target:** Always above the calorie floor
- **Smart direction:** If you're losing slower than expected, the app may reduce your target

> 💡 **Key Idea**
> Small, frequent adjustments are more sustainable than large, infrequent ones. A 200 kcal tweak weekly for three weeks gets you to the right place faster than a 600 kcal swing all at once.

## What Causes a Change

- **Weight loss stalls despite adherence** — TDEE may have decreased. Target reduced.
- **Weight loss faster than planned** — You have more room. Target increased.
- **New to the app** — TDEE stabilizing. Targets may shift more initially.
- **Logging break (3+ days)** — TDEE freezes until you resume.
- **Goal change** — Full recalculation.

## The Confidence Indicator

On the Strategy screen, confidence reflects:

- **Coverage** — % of days logged in past 14 days
- **Freshness** — How recent your last log was
- **Stability** — Consistency of patterns
- **Depth** — How much history feeds the estimate

Low confidence = less certainty about TDEE. Log more consistently and it improves.

> 📊 **In MacroPhase**
> The combination of dynamic TDEE and capped weekly adjustments means your plan evolves with you automatically. You don't need to manually recalculate — just log and check in.

## What If I Skip Check-Ins?

Your targets stay unchanged. The TDEE updates in the background, but calorie and macro numbers don't change until you complete a check-in or adjust manually.

> ⚠️ **Common Myth**
> "The app is changing my calories without telling me." It doesn't. Adjustments only happen during check-ins, which you approve. Between check-ins, your targets are locked.
