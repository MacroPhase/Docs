# Smart Shopping & Receipt Scanner

MacroPhase includes a local-first Smart Shopping system and an AI-powered Receipt Scanner that links your grocery shopping directly to your nutrition planning.

## The Smart Shopping List

Your shopping list is built to keep your nutrition goals and real-world grocery runs in sync:

- **Local-First & Offline**: Your lists, pantry items, and purchase history live directly in your local database.
- **Categorized Groceries**: Items are automatically grouped into clean supermarket sections (Produce, Meat & Seafood, Dairy, Bakery, Pantry, Frozen, Beverages, Snacks, etc.) for quick in-store shopping.
- **Smart Restocking Suggestions**: MacroPhase analyzes your recent logged foods and meal routines to proactively suggest staple items before you run out.
- **Pantry & Stock Management**: Mark items as bought to move them to your pantry inventory or tap to restock.

## AI Receipt Scanner

Instead of manually typing grocery items, you can scan printed grocery receipts with your camera or import photos from your gallery:

1. **Snap or Upload**: Take a clear photo of your paper receipt or upload an invoice from your grocery app.
2. **AI Line Item Extraction**: The scanner reads the receipt, extracting:
   - Product names and cleaned descriptions
   - Quantities and units (grams, kg, packs, liters)
   - Item prices and currency (EUR, USD, GBP, SEK, KRW, etc.)
   - Category classifications
3. **Batch Approval**: Review the extracted items, adjust quantities or names with inline edits, and approve all items in a single tap.
4. **Instant Custom Food Creation**: Convert any purchased item on the receipt into a logged Custom Food with pre-filled brand and serving data.

> 💡 **Tip**
> If a receipt is long or folded, take a photo with good lighting and flattened edges. The AI OCR grounds text bounding boxes to ensure item lines and prices match accurately.

## AI Coach Integration

Your AI Coach has direct tools to interact with your shopping list:

- Ask: *"Add the ingredients for tonight's chicken stir-fry recipe to my shopping list"*
- Ask: *"What groceries do I need to buy this week to hit 180g protein daily?"*
- Ask: *"Clear the checked items from my shopping list"*

The coach will read your meal targets, check what's missing, and populate your grocery list automatically.
